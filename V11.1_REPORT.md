# DABSy v11.1 — recovery report

## Bugs, root causes, fixes
**1 Keyboard lag.** Causes found in the code: (a) the chat input was focused in a 340 ms `setTimeout`, so Android raised the keyboard late and outside the tap; (b) `viewport-engine.js` ran a *smooth* `scrollIntoView` plus chat-log scrolling on every keyboard resize (fighting the resize), wrote `--vvh` on every frame, and queued extra 120/300 ms re-measures; (c) `#chat-area` animated its `bottom` (layout every frame); (d) backdrop blur and ambient loops (breathing, eye glow, lamp glow) kept running behind the keyboard.
Fix: focus inside the tap; viewport engine now does one rAF-coalesced, layout-read-free measure, writes `--kb` only when it moves ≥2 px and `--vvh` once after it settles, and never scrolls; no `bottom` transition; while chat is open blur is off (solid dock surfaces) and ambient loops are paused. Side effect: a *double*-tap on her eyes (apps) may briefly flash the keyboard. Apps also have the visible shortcut.

**2 Can't plan the day.** Root cause: "plan my day" matched the local "my day" command (opened the empty task list) and then the butler overview ("nothing on your list"). New `dayplan-engine.js` runs first and builds a real schedule from the clock, tasks, calendar events (if connected), free time, priorities, breaks (draft with assumptions shown). Plan card: reorder, ±15 min, remove, add item, Accept & save / Reject. Accept writes through `tasks.add/update`, reads every record back and reports "saved N of M" honestly, with Undo. With nothing known it asks ONE question (and accepts a typed list). Also handled: what next, "I have 30 minutes", exam prep (asks date only if not found on calendar/tasks), organise week, move unfinished to tomorrow (with Undo), remind me later (real task reminder, with Undo). The Gemini persona prompt now carries the butler rules + real facts and forbids claiming saves it can't make.

**3 Wallpapers missing.** Root cause: the project only ever contained ONE room (night); no day art or other wallpapers existed in the repo (`habitats-data.js` had `day:null`). Your five pictures are now built in: Window nook (day+night), Lakeside (day+night), City window (day only). Picker with previews in Settings → Magic World; choice persists (`world_v1`). Paths are relative; each file loads from `assets/magic/` and falls back to its embedded `wp-*.js` copy if folders were skipped on upload.

**4 Day mode.** Auto now follows the local clock (06:30–18:30) and switches at the boundary (timer + re-check on return to the app). Interface theme "Auto" also follows the clock, no longer the phone's dark mode. Manual Day/Night kept.

**5 Widgets.** New `widget-engine.js`: clock, date, tasks (real), calendar (Ghibli, real; "connect" prompt if not), weather (Open-Meteo free, only after you tap to allow location; shows "unavailable" otherwise). Float in the upper area, appear one by one, hide during chat/panels/Study Mode. Press-hold or Settings → Widgets or "customise widgets": drag to a slot, ✕ remove, add back; saved. Resize intentionally not offered.

**6 Personal butler.** Items 2 and 5 plus the butler persona; actions are distinguished from suggestions and are verified before being claimed.
