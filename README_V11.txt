DABSy v11.1 — quick start (phone, no coding)
1. Unzip. Upload everything in the folder to your GitHub Pages repo (replace old files). Folders are optional-safe:
   every picture also exists as a root-level wp-*.js file, so a flat upload still shows all wallpapers.
2. Open your DABSy link, wait a few seconds, refresh once (the service-worker cache is now dabsy-v11.1.0).
3. Settings -> Magic World -> choose Magic World, then pick a Wallpaper. Settings -> Widgets to customise.
Try: "plan my day", "what should I do next?", "I have 30 minutes free", "help me prepare for my physics exam",
"organise my week", "move my unfinished tasks to tomorrow", "remind me about this later", "customise widgets".
Backend/keys are unchanged and live only in Cloudflare. Details: V11.1_REPORT.md
